The endpoint mainnet-rpc.avail.so is provided and maintained by the Avail Project itself, i.e. it’s an official RPC endpoint run directly by the core Avail team.

This is confirmed in the official Avail documentation (last updated May 18, 2025), where it’s listed under “RPC Endpoints” for Mainnet alongside partner endpoints  ￼


Here’s a detailed breakdown of the major hosted Avail mainnet RPC providers, why they exist, and how they price their services:

  ⸻
  
  🚀 1. BlastAPI (by Bware Labs)
  
  Who they are:
  Bware Labs offers BlastAPI, a decentralized RPC infrastructure powered by geographically distributed nodes  ￼ ￼. Reddit users highlight its multi-region architecture, high performance, and affordability:
  
  “BlastAPI is transforming the landscape by offering developers a comprehensive and powerful platform…operates on a permissionless basis”  ￼
  “…subscription-based API model offers developers flexibility and cost‑effectiveness”  ￼
  
  Why they provide RPC:
  To support scalable, low-latency blockchain access for dApps, benefiting from decentralized node hosting and simplifying developer onboarding.
  
  Pricing & Tiers:
    •	Free Public Tier: Generous throughput (~633 requests/sec recent average)  ￼
    •	Paid/Consumer Plans: Offer dedicated endpoints, higher throughput, archive support starting Developer tier ($49/mo)  ￼
  
  ⸻
  
  🌐 2. Ankr
  
  Who they are:
  Ankr is a global Web3 infrastructure platform—offering both free public and premium RPC services across 60+ chains  ￼.
  
  Why they provide RPC:
  To offer scalable, reliable infrastructure with decentralization and support for both casual and enterprise-grade use.
  
  Pricing Structure:
    •	Free Tier: Up to ~30 requests/sec, community support  ￼
    •	Premium (Pay‑As‑You‑Go): $0.00002 per HTTPS request (200 credits at $0.10 per 1M credits) ($0.02/1k requests), supports up to ~1,500 rps, plus WebSocket subscriptions  ￼
    •	Enterprise Plan: Custom contracts, SLAs, very high throughput  ￼
  
  Notable Features:
  Archive data access, advanced analytics, team support, global node distribution with low latency  ￼.
  
  ⸻
  
  🛠️ 3. OnFinality
  
  Who they are:
  A SaaS platform providing managed blockchain infrastructure and indexing services for developers and enterprises  ￼.
  
  Why they provide RPC:
  To simplify node deployment, monitoring, and scaling, saving developers the overhead of maintaining blockchain infrastructure.
  
  Pricing Model:
    •	Free Tier: Limited access
    •	Standard Plan: ~$0.08/hour + extras for compute units, chain support, and storage entities  ￼ (~$58–$490/month depending on scale)
    •	Enterprise: Custom, 99.9% SLA, support included

🔍 Why So Many Providers?
	•	Redundancy & Performance: Developers can choose low-latency endpoints near them.
	•	Free vs Paid Options: Many providers offer free public access, with paid tiers for higher throughput and SLAs.
	•	Monetization: Infrastructure support helps these providers fund their validator nodes or staking services.
